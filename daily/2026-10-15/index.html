    Reclaiming Low-Quality Instruction Data with Rule-Guided LLM Reconstruction

    arXiv:2510.23160v2 · 论文 PDF

    作者：Zile Yang；Ling Li；Na Di；Jinlong Pang；Yao Zhou；Hao Cheng；Bo Han；Jiaheng Wei

    公告日期：2026-10-15；更新类型：替换版本；阅读版本：v2

    标签：主题=拟合与蒸馏；机构类型=高校；层级=垂类

    作者单位：The Hong Kong University of Science and Technology (Guangzhou)；University of California, Santa Cruz；Hong Kong Baptist University

    单位分类：1=高校；2=高校；3=高校

    问题：监督微调的数据选择方法通常依据质量、难度、多样性等标准保留高分指令样本，而丢弃低分样本；但低分样本可能仍包含不完整问题、不足答案或冗余内容中保留的有用概念与上下文信息，直接丢弃会损失潜在监督信号。论文研究能否从本会被选择方法丢弃的低分指令数据中重建有用监督，作为选择式数据策展的补充路径。

    方法：提出规则引导的LLM重建框架，分三阶段。阶段1用GPT-4o-mini按DS2提示对稀有性、复杂度、信息量、总体评分四维各1-10分评分，再映射为0-5质量分，并用噪声标签转移矩阵T_{i,j}=P(ỹ=j|y=i)校正评分，按校正分划分低分集（0-2）与高分集（3-5）。阶段2以余弦相似度≥0.9形成一跳簇，单例簇保留，可分簇用平均轮廓系数在2到min(10,|C'|-1)范围内选k-means子簇数，每子簇≥3样本时选距质心最近代表r0与MMR启发代表rMMR（α=0.2），否则全保留。阶段3先做结构依赖过滤排除代码、表格、格式敏感数学表达式，再对源对做领域分析（同域/相关域/无关域），每类关系对应3种预定义策略共9种，MCG生成候选，ICD检查关键术语覆盖、问题质量、上下文完整性与答案完整性，失败时RPU映射预定义修复指令并重生成（最多3次候选）；Cycle 2仅精炼回答，FAC检查直接性与冗余，失败时FAU修复。终止条件为全部验证通过或达迭代上限，否则选未满足条件最少者。

    实验范围：源池300,932条，来自Stanford Alpaca约52K、Flan v2约100K、OpenAssistant 1约33K、WizardLM约100K、Dolly约15K；LQ Set 123,786条（校正分0-2），HQ Set 131,247条（3-5），重建54,888条（intra-cluster 15,488、inter-cluster 39,400）。基座为Mistral-7B-v0.3、LLaMA-3.1-8B、Qwen2.5-7B；GPT-4o-mini用于评分与重建，DA温度0.4、其他算子0.2。评测MMLU、TruthfulQA、GSM8K、BBH、TyDiQA，Mistral与Llama评五基准，Qwen不含TruthfulQA。基线包括Completion Length、KNN10、Perplexity、Random、AlpaGasus、IFD、Superfiltering、DEITA、RDS+、DS2、LESS及Self-Instruct、MathFusion等。主表报告：Mistral五基准平均52.2±0.9，高于LQ Set 45.7（+6.5）、HQ Set 50.3、Full Set 48.7±2.6，最强选择基线LESS 49.7；Llama 57.4±0.3，高于LQ Set 51.8（+5.6）、HQ Set 56.0、Full Set 56.8±1.3，最强基线Completion Length 57.0；Qwen四基准平均69.3，高于LQ 65.2、HQ 68.0、Full 68.8。消融：Mistral引入Step2从44.1±0.5升至45.6±0.3，加入Step3升至52.2±0.9；Llama从51.2±0.3到51.9±0.6再到57.4±0.3。数据规模20%/60%/100%下Mistral四基准平均51.2±2.1→51.9±2.0→52.9±1.1，Llama 57.6±1.8→58.8±0.6→59.9±0.5。人工审计30样本：上下文完整性93.3%、问题清晰度93.3%、来源整合66.7%、答案忠实60.0%、整体可用46.7%、五项全过46.7%；成对簇间重构来源整合11/13（84.6%）高于多源簇内9/17（52.9%）。成本：Ours平均API 0.005美元、GPU 17小时、不需验证集；LESS 0美元、152.5小时、需验证集。附录Table 19报告 HQ+Ours 下 Llama 57.7，与 Full 57.7 持平；Qwen 63.1，低于 Full 63.3。不同表格和组合的结果需分别报告。

    判断：论文提出规则引导的LLM重建框架，将低分指令数据转化为可回收监督来源。三个7B/8B基座使用约18.2%的样本量，报告平均分高于低分源集、高分集、全量集及多数数据选择基线；逐步消融将相对直接融合的最大增益归于规则引导重建，支持其作为选择式SFT数据策展的补充路径。证据限制包括：校正分用于数据划分，论文未以独立真值验证其质量估计；Step1贡献未被单独隔离，Step2与Step3联合评估；重复SFT反映固定重建数据集下的训练变异，未系统变化评分或生成模型；人工审计覆盖54,888个重建样本中的30个，所得比例描述该抽样设置，不能作为全数据集精确估计，论文也未报告校正偶然一致性的标注者间一致性统计。审计显示来源整合66.7%、答案忠实60.0%、整体可用46.7%；失败样例涉及遗漏源信息、无支撑主张、任务漂移与内部不一致。框架面向自然语言指令数据；代码、表格、格式敏感数学表达式等结构依赖输入需要先过滤，增加了重构限制。方法依赖外部LLM的多次 API 调用，具有离线构建成本和模型敏感性。重构可能改变任务与领域组成，观测到的增益同时反映这些组成变化。主表与附录表在 HQ/Full 设置下呈现不同结果，结论应按对应实验条件表述。

    原文摘要：Data selection for supervised fine-tuning (SFT) often discards low-scoring instruction examples that may retain useful supervision. We propose a rule-guided LLM reconstruction framework to reclaim this information. The framework identifies low-scoring samples through LLM-based rating and score correction, selects semantically representative sources, and reconstructs instruction-response pairs through strategy-guided generation, criteria-based validation, and failure-guided repair. From 123,786 low-scoring samples, it constructs 54,888 training examples. Across three 7B/8B base LLMs, the reconstructed data achieves higher average downstream performance than the low-scoring source set and all evaluated data-selection baselines. Despite using substantially fewer examples than the full source dataset (approximately 300K), it achieves higher reported average scores than full-data training across all three base models. Stepwise ablations identify rule-guided reconstruction as the largest source of observed improvements over direct fusion, while a manual audit reveals remaining limitations in source integration and answer faithfulness. These findings support reconstruction as a complementary approach to selection-based SFT data curation.

    关键证据：

    • 研究问题：选择式数据策展丢弃低分样本，本文探索能否从本会被丢弃的样本中重建有用监督。：This motivates a complementary per- spective on SFT data curation: rather than only asking which samples should be re- tained, can useful supervision be recon- structed from samples that would other- wise be discarded?
    • 方法机制：三阶段框架，包括低分识别、语义分组与代表选择、规则引导LLM重建。：Our framework reconstructs instruction- response pairs from low-scoring source data through three stages, as illustrated in Figure 2.
    • 训练设定与数据规模：源池300,932条，LQ Set 123,786条，重建54,888条，其中intra-cluster 15,488、inter-cluster 39,400。：Our framework recon- structs 54,888 training samples from the LQ Set (123,786 samples), comprising 15,488 intra-cluster and 39,400 inter-cluster samples.
    • 结果：规则引导重建替代直接一步融合后，Mistral-7B-v0.3五基准平均从45.6±0.3升至52.2±0.9，Llama-3.1-8B从51.9±0.6升至57.4±0.3。：Stepwise ablations further show that replac- ing direct one-step fusion with rule-guided re- construction increases the five-benchmark av- erage from 45.6 ± 0.3 to 52.2 ± 0.9 for Mistral- 7B-v0.3 and from 51.9 ± 0.6 to 57.4 ± 0.3 for Llama-3.1-8B.
    • Mistral与Llama上本方法平均分及相对LQ Set增益。：our method achieves the highest re- ported five-benchmark averages for Mistral (52.2 ± 0.9) and Llama (57.4 ± 0.3), exceeding the strongest data-selection baselines, LESS (49.7) and Completion Length (57.0), respec- tively. Compared with the single-run LQ Set baselines, our two-seed mean scores are higher by 6.5 and 5.6 points.
    • Qwen2.5-7B四基准平均：Ours 69.3，LQ 65.2，HQ 68.0，Full 68.8，分别提升4.1、1.3、0.5。：Our reconstructed dataset achieves the high- est reported four-benchmark average of 69.3, compared with 65.2 for the LQ Set, 68.0 for the HQ Set, and 68.8 for the Full Set.     • 审计局限：仅覆盖54,888个重建样本中的30个，未独立验证所有继承源主张真值，且未报告校正偶然一致性的标注者间一致性统计。：First, it covers only 30 samples out of 54,888 reconstructed examples; criterion-wise rates and differences between reconstruction settings are descriptive rather than precise estimates for the full dataset. Second, the audit does not independently verify the truth of all inherited source claims. Third, although each reported binary label is supported by strict-majority consensus among multiple human annotators, we do not report a chance-corrected inter-annotator agreement statistic.
    • 结构依赖过滤排除代码、表格、格式敏感数学表达式等。：Specifically, samples containing executable code with syntax or indentation dependen- cies, tables with essential row-column relation- ships, format-sensitive mathematical expres- sions, or other structure-dependent content are excluded when altering their original rep- resentations may change their meaning or cor- rectness.

    The Teacher Is a Direction, Not a Destination: Extrapolating RL-Induced Representation Residuals in On-Policy Distillation

    arXiv:2609.36484v2 · 论文 PDF

    作者：Hao Li；MeiJia Chen；Weijie Ren；Donghan Li；Zijun Tian；Jingchun Huang；Naibo Wang

    公告日期：2026-10-15；更新类型：替换版本；阅读版本：v2

    标签：主题=语言模型在策略蒸馏（On-Policy Distillation, OPD）；机构类型=高校+个人；层级=垂类

    作者单位：University of Science and Technology of China；Rutgers University；Zhejiang University；Independent Researcher

    单位分类：1=高校；2=高校；3=高校；4=个人

    问题：在策略蒸馏（OPD）中，学生模型在自身轨迹上匹配教师下一 token 分布，广义变体通过输出空间外推隐式奖励使学生超越教师。语言模型头会各向异性地衰减此类变化：512 个最弱方向承载教师-基座残差隐藏态能量的 79.8%，并承载中心化 logit 能量的 30.0%。输出空间目标因此对多数残差方向提供的监督较弱，也难以约束早期层；采样 token 对数概率比会注入噪声，外推过程放大该噪声，造成训练不稳定。

    方法：提出 RIDE（RL-Induced Direction Extrapolation），在表示空间直接外推 RL 诱导变化。学生初始化于教师 RL 前 checkpoint，三模型共享表示空间；在每个层和 token 位置计算教师与 RL 前 checkpoint 的隐藏状态残差 Δ，将学生隐藏状态回归到沿该残差超出教师的目标 h*=hT+(λ−1)Δ=λhT+(1−λ)hB。该回归等价于在教师为中心的二次惩罚下最大化线性方向奖励 r(h)=⟨h−hT,Δ⟩，即 (λ−1)r(h)−½||h−hT||²。训练监督所有 L 层和最后 k=2000 响应位置，λ=1.25，损失按 λ⁻² 缩放；λ=1 恢复 OPRD。

    原文摘要：On-policy distillation (OPD) trains a student to match the teacher's next-token distributions on the student's own trajectories and has yielded substantial empirical gains. Generalized variants allow the student to surpass the teacher by extrapolating an implicit reward in output space. The language-model head, however, attenuates this change anisotropically: much of the change encoded in the teacher's hidden states reaches the logits at a small fraction of its weight, and the sampled-token log-probability ratios on which output-space extrapolation relies inject noise that the extrapolation amplifies, making training unstable. We observe that reinforcement learning (RL) shifts a model's internal representations relative to its base checkpoint, and that the direction of this shift can be measured at every layer. Motivated by this observation, we propose RIDE (RL-Induced Direction Extrapolation), which extrapolates the RL-induced change directly in representation space: at every layer and token position, RIDE computes the residual between the teacher and its pre-RL checkpoint and regresses the student's hidden states toward targets displaced beyond the teacher along this residual. Conditioned on a sampled trajectory, this regression is equivalent to maximizing a linear directional reward defined by the residual under a quadratic penalty centered at the teacher, which makes explicit how the objective moves the student along the RL-induced direction while limiting its deviation from the teacher.
