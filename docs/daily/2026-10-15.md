## 拟合与蒸馏

<article markdown="1" class="daily-paper-card" data-topic="拟合与蒸馏" data-org="单位层级待核实" data-coverage="任务内通用" data-task="监督微调指令数据策展">

### Reclaiming Low-Quality Instruction Data with Rule-Guided LLM Reconstruction

[arXiv:2510.23160v2](https://arxiv.org/abs/2510.23160v2) · [论文 PDF](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2510.23160v2)

**作者**：Zile Yang；Ling Li；Na Di；Jinlong Pang；Yao Zhou；Hao Cheng；Bo Han；Jiaheng Wei

**公告日期**：2026-10-15；**更新类型**：替换版本；**阅读版本**：v2

**标签**：主题=`拟合与蒸馏`；机构组合=`单位层级待核实`；覆盖面=`任务内通用`；任务类型=`监督微调指令数据策展`

**作者单位**：The Hong Kong University of Science and Technology (Guangzhou)；University of California, Santa Cruz；Hong Kong Baptist University

**问题**：监督微调的数据选择方法通常依据质量、难度、多样性等标准保留高分指令样本，而丢弃低分样本；但低分样本可能仍包含不完整问题、不足答案或冗余内容中保留的有用概念与上下文信息，直接丢弃会损失潜在监督信号。论文研究能否从本会被选择方法丢弃的低分指令数据中重建有用监督，作为选择式数据策展的补充路径。

**方法**：提出规则引导的LLM重建框架，分三阶段。阶段1用GPT-4o-mini按DS2提示对稀有性、复杂度、信息量、总体评分四维各1-10分评分，再映射为0-5质量分，并用噪声标签转移矩阵T_{i,j}=P(ỹ=j|y=i)校正评分，按校正分划分低分集（0-2）与高分集（3-5）。阶段2以余弦相似度≥0.9形成一跳簇，单例簇保留，可分簇用平均轮廓系数在2到min(10,|C'|-1)范围内选k-means子簇数，每子簇≥3样本时选距质心最近代表r0与MMR启发代表rMMR（α=0.2），否则全保留。阶段3先做结构依赖过滤排除代码、表格、格式敏感数学表达式，再对源对做领域分析（同域/相关域/无关域），每类关系对应3种预定义策略共9种，MCG生成候选，ICD检查关键术语覆盖、问题质量、上下文完整性与答案完整性，失败时RPU映射预定义修复指令并重生成（最多3次候选）；Cycle 2仅精炼回答，FAC检查直接性与冗余，失败时FAU修复。终止条件为全部验证通过或达迭代上限，否则选未满足条件最少者。

**实验范围**：源池300,932条，来自Stanford Alpaca约52K、Flan v2约100K、OpenAssistant 1约33K、WizardLM约100K、Dolly约15K；LQ Set 123,786条（校正分0-2），HQ Set 131,247条（3-5），重建54,888条（intra-cluster 15,488、inter-cluster 39,400）。基座为Mistral-7B-v0.3、LLaMA-3.1-8B、Qwen2.5-7B；GPT-4o-mini用于评分与重建，DA温度0.4、其他算子0.2。评测MMLU、TruthfulQA、GSM8K、BBH、TyDiQA，Mistral与Llama评五基准，Qwen不含TruthfulQA。基线包括Completion Length、KNN10、Perplexity、Random、AlpaGasus、IFD、Superfiltering、DEITA、RDS+、DS2、LESS及Self-Instruct、MathFusion等。主表报告：Mistral五基准平均52.2±0.9，高于LQ Set 45.7（+6.5）、HQ Set 50.3、Full Set 48.7±2.6，最强选择基线LESS 49.7；Llama 57.4±0.3，高于LQ Set 51.8（+5.6）、HQ Set 56.0、Full Set 56.8±1.3，最强基线Completion Length 57.0；Qwen四基准平均69.3，高于LQ 65.2、HQ 68.0、Full 68.8。消融：Mistral引入Step2从44.1±0.5升至45.6±0.3，加入Step3升至52.2±0.9；Llama从51.2±0.3到51.9±0.6再到57.4±0.3。数据规模20%/60%/100%下Mistral四基准平均51.2±2.1→51.9±2.0→52.9±1.1，Llama 57.6±1.8→58.8±0.6→59.9±0.5。人工审计30样本：上下文完整性93.3%、问题清晰度93.3%、来源整合66.7%、答案忠实60.0%、整体可用46.7%、五项全过46.7%；成对簇间重构来源整合11/13（84.6%）高于多源簇内9/17（52.9%）。成本：Ours平均API 0.005美元、GPU 17小时、不需验证集；LESS 0美元、152.5小时、需验证集。需注意附录Table 19在HQ+Ours组合下Llama 57.7仅持平Full 57.7、Qwen 63.1低于Full 63.3，故“超过HQ与Full Set”并非所有设置成立。

**判断**：论文提出规则引导的LLM重建框架，将低分指令数据从被丢弃对象转化为可回收监督来源，在三个7B/8B基座上以约18.2%的样本量取得高于低分源集、高分集、全量集及多数数据选择基线的报告平均分，逐步消融显示规则引导重建是相对直接融合的最大增益来源，支持重建作为选择式SFT数据策展的补充路径。但证据存在若干局限：校正分是在噪声与可聚类假设下用于数据划分的估计，并非独立验证的真实质量标注；Step1贡献未被独立隔离，Step2与Step3联合评估；重复SFT仅反映固定重建数据集下的训练变异，未系统变化评分或生成模型；人工审计仅覆盖54,888个重建样本中的30个，比率与设置差异为描述性而非全数据集精确估计，且未报告校正偶然一致性的标注者间一致性统计；审计发现来源整合66.7%、答案忠实60.0%、整体可用46.7%，失败模式包括遗漏源信息、引入无支撑主张、任务漂移与内部不一致；框架主要面向自然语言指令数据，代码、表格、格式敏感数学表达式等结构依赖输入需先过滤，重构困难；依赖外部LLM多次API调用，存在离线构建成本与模型敏感性；重构可能改变任务与领域组成，改进不能仅归因于质量提升；主表与附录表在HQ/Full设置下存在不同结果，部分概括需限定条件。

**原文摘要**：Data selection for supervised fine-tuning (SFT) often discards low-scoring instruction examples that may retain useful supervision. We propose a rule-guided LLM reconstruction framework to reclaim this information. The framework identifies low-scoring samples through LLM-based rating and score correction, selects semantically representative sources, and reconstructs instruction-response pairs through strategy-guided generation, criteria-based validation, and failure-guided repair. From 123,786 low-scoring samples, it constructs 54,888 training examples. Across three 7B/8B base LLMs, the reconstructed data achieves higher average downstream performance than the low-scoring source set and all evaluated data-selection baselines. Despite using substantially fewer examples than the full source dataset (approximately 300K), it achieves higher reported average scores than full-data training across all three base models. Stepwise ablations identify rule-guided reconstruction as the largest source of observed improvements over direct fusion, while a manual audit reveals remaining limitations in source integration and answer faithfulness. These findings support reconstruction as a complementary approach to selection-based SFT data curation.

## 语言模型在策略蒸馏（On-Policy Distillation, OPD）

<article markdown="1" class="daily-paper-card" data-topic="语言模型在策略蒸馏（On-Policy Distillation, OPD）" data-org="单位层级待核实" data-coverage="任务内通用" data-task="语言模型在策略蒸馏">

### The Teacher Is a Direction, Not a Destination: Extrapolating RL-Induced Representation Residuals in On-Policy Distillation

[arXiv:2609.36484v2](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.36484v2) · [论文 PDF](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2609.36484v2)

**作者**：Hao Li；MeiJia Chen；Weijie Ren；Donghan Li；Zijun Tian；Jingchun Huang；Naibo Wang

**公告日期**：2026-10-15；**更新类型**：替换版本；**阅读版本**：v2

**标签**：主题=`语言模型在策略蒸馏（On-Policy Distillation, OPD）`；机构组合=`单位层级待核实`；覆盖面=`任务内通用`；任务类型=`语言模型在策略蒸馏`

**作者单位**：University of Science and Technology of China；Rutgers University；Zhejiang University；Independent Researcher

**问题**：在策略蒸馏（OPD）训练学生模型在学生自身轨迹上匹配教师下一 token 分布，广义变体通过在输出空间外推隐式奖励使学生超越教师。但语言模型头各向异性地衰减该变化：512 个最弱方向承载教师-基座残差隐藏态能量的 79.8%，却仅承载中心化 logit 能量的 30.0%，导致输出空间目标对大部分残差监督微弱且不约束早期层；采样 token 对数概率比还注入噪声并被外推放大，造成训练不稳定。

**方法**：提出 RIDE（RL-Induced Direction Extrapolation），在表示空间直接外推 RL 诱导变化。学生初始化于教师 RL 前 checkpoint，三模型共享表示空间；在每个层和 token 位置计算教师与 RL 前 checkpoint 的隐藏状态残差 Δ，将学生隐藏状态回归到沿该残差超出教师的目标 h*=hT+(λ−1)Δ=λhT+(1−λ)hB。该回归等价于在教师为中心的二次惩罚下最大化线性方向奖励 r(h)=⟨h−hT,Δ⟩，即 (λ−1)r(h)−½||h−hT||²。训练监督所有 L 层和最后 k=2000 响应位置，λ=1.25，损失按 λ⁻² 缩放；λ=1 恢复 OPRD。

**实验范围**：在四对 base/RL-teacher 上评估：R1-Distill-1.5B→JustRL-1.5B、Qwen3-4B→Just-Qwen3-4B、Llama-3.2-3B→Just-Llama-3.2-3B、Phi-4-mini→Just-Phi-4-mini，数据 DAPO-Math-17K，训练 500 步，每方法三个种子（14、42、2027）。在 AIME24、AIME25、AIMO 上以 Avg@16 评估。RIDE 在四对均接近或超过 RL 教师（+1.08、+0.48、+0.32、+0.34 分），是唯一均值超过教师的方法，超 OPRD 0.97–4.06 点，平均超 ExOPD 9.1 点；ExOPD 在每对均低于教师。λ 扫描中 RIDE 在 λ=1.25 峰值 55.4，0.75≤λ≤1.35 格式分 94–97%。

**判断**：RIDE 通过在表示空间沿 RL 诱导残差方向外推，规避了语言模型头的各向异性衰减与输出空间对数比噪声，在四个不同规模、架构和预训练谱系的 base/RL-teacher 对上一致优于 OPRD 与输出空间外推，且是唯一均值超过 RL 教师的方法。消融显示随机、反向、错配来源与轨迹错配控制均在一分内，支持残差方向的有效性。局限：需 pre-RL 检查点与共享表示空间，使用单一全局 λ，仅在数学推理与一种 RL 配方上评估；学生被监督于无模型实际产生的隐状态目标，校准与安全行为需独立评估，可能放大教师偏见或错误。

**原文摘要**：On-policy distillation (OPD) trains a student to match the teacher's next-token distributions on the student's own trajectories and has yielded substantial empirical gains. Generalized variants allow the student to surpass the teacher by extrapolating an implicit reward in output space. The language-model head, however, attenuates this change anisotropically: much of the change encoded in the teacher's hidden states reaches the logits at a small fraction of its weight, and the sampled-token log-probability ratios on which output-space extrapolation relies inject noise that the extrapolation amplifies, making training unstable. We observe that reinforcement learning (RL) shifts a model's internal representations relative to its base checkpoint, and that the direction of this shift can be measured at every layer. Motivated by this observation, we propose RIDE (RL-Induced Direction Extrapolation), which extrapolates the RL-induced change directly in representation space: at every layer and token position, RIDE computes the residual between the teacher and its pre-RL checkpoint and regresses the student's hidden states toward targets displaced beyond the teacher along this residual. Conditioned on a sampled trajectory, this regression is equivalent to maximizing a linear directional reward defined by the residual under a quadratic penalty centered at the teacher, which makes explicit how the objective moves the student along the RL-induced direction while limiting its deviation from the teacher. Across four base/RL-teacher pairs spanning different scales, architectures, and pre-training lineages, RIDE approaches or exceeds the RL-trained teacher on every pair and is the only method whose mean does so, and it consistently outperforms output-space extrapolation, which degrades the student whenever the teacher is close to its base. Project page: this https URL .

**关键证据**：

- RIDE 在表示空间外推 RL 诱导残差，目标为教师加(λ−1)倍残差：RIDE computes the residual between the teacher and its pre-RL checkpoint and regresses the student’s hidden states toward targets displaced beyond the teacher along this residual.
- 语言模型头各向异性地衰减残差，512个最弱方向承载79.8%隐藏态能量但仅30.0%中心化logit能量：its 512 weakest directions carry 79.8% of the teacher-to-base residual’s hidden-state energy but only 30.0% of its centered-logit energy
- RIDE 在四对 base/RL-teacher 上均接近或超过教师，是唯一均值超过教师的方法：RIDE approaches or exceeds the RL-trained teacher on every pair and is the only method whose mean does so
- 训练设定：RIDE 监督所有层和最后 k=2000 响应位置，λ=1.25，损失按 λ−2 缩放：RIDE supervises all L layers and the last k = 2,000 response positions with λ = 1.25 and loss scaling by λ−2
- RIDE是唯一均值高于教师线的方法，且其相对OPRD的边际隔离了残差外推的收益：RIDE is the only method whose mean lies above the teacher line, on every pair, although on three pairs the margin is within one across-seed standard deviation (Table 4); all teacher-matching meth-ods remain below the line, as their objectives dictate, and output-space extrapolation falls below it as well. Since RIDE differs from OPRD only in its target, its margin over OPRD (0.97 to 4.06 points) isolates the benefit of residual extrapolation under a shared protocol.
- ExOPD外推优势噪声按(λ−1)²缩放，教师到基座差距小时损害最大：This is the behavior predicted by Proposition 4.1: the extrapolated component of the sampled advantage is scaled by (λ−1)2 regardless of how much signal the log-ratio carries, and the damage is largest when the teacher-to-base gap is small.
- RIDE的λ扫描峰值与格式分、响应长度变化：For RIDE, final Avg@16 rises with λ from 48.2 at λ = 0.5 to 54.3 at 1 and peaks at 55.4 at 1.25; the neighboring coefficients λ = 1.15 and 1.35 give 55.2 and 55.3, so every λ ∈ \[1.15, 1.35\] improves over OPRD (full numbers in Section B). ### Rubric Dropout: A Simple Way to Mitigate Reward Hacking in Rubric-as-Reward RL

[arXiv:2608.11669v2](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.11669v2) · [论文 PDF](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2608.11669v2)

**作者**：Minglai Yang；Xinyu Guo；Zhengliang Shi；Utkarsh Tyagi；Mian Zhang；Razvan Dumitru；Sunjie Hou；Yunzhong He；Daniel Yue Zhang；Ying Liu；Dan Roth

**公告日期**：2026-10-15；**更新类型**：替换版本；**阅读版本**：v2

**标签**：主题=`奖励强化学习`；机构组合=`单位层级待核实`；覆盖面=`任务内通用`；任务类型=`语言模型`

**作者单位**：Scale AI；University of Arizona；Carnegie Mellon University；University of Texas at Dallas；University of Pennsylvania

**问题**：基于评分标准（rubric）的强化学习中，LLM 评判器按提示特定标准打分，但 rubric 只是响应质量的代理，策略长期针对固定代理优化会学会利用代理与真实质量之间的差异，即奖励黑客。论文在医学与科学 rubric 上用 GRPO 训练模型，并以训练评判与更强的 OOD 金评判在分布外基准上持续评分，观察到两者分数先同升后分叉：金分上升、达峰后下降，而代理分持续上升，被过度声称的标准集合不断扩大。

**方法**：提出 Rubric Dropout：在每个训练步随机丢弃每条 rubric 标准的一个子集，使被奖励标准子集每步重采样；同一提示的所有 rollout 用同一子集评分以保证奖励可比，评估始终使用完整 rubric。基础奖励 R(x,y)=Σ w_k s_k(x,y)/Σ w_k（式1），dropout 奖励 R̃(x,y;m)=Σ m_k w_k s_k(x,y)/Σ m_k w_k（式2），m 为保留掩码。还提出 Block Dropout，将语义相似标准成组一起丢弃，奖励 R_M(x,y)=Σ_{k∈K_M} w_k s_k(x,y)/Σ_{k∈K_M} w_k（式3），K_M 为保留块并集，防止近似重复标准继续奖励同一行为。理论分析表明组共享掩码下任何仅依赖掩码的正规化因子在组标准化优势中约去，dropout 期望上只带来全局缩放 1−f 并被组标准化移除，故仅通过方差项起作用，方差在优势依赖单一高权重标准时最大，f(1−f) 在 f=1/2 最大。

**实验范围**：训练使用 GRPO，每提示16个 rollout，学习率1e-6，模型为 Qwen3 1.7B 至 8B；主实验为 Qwen3-8B，两对 train→eval：RubricHub-Medical→HealthBench-Hard 与 RubricHub-Science→ResearchQA；代理评判 gpt-4o-mini，金评判 claude-sonnet-4-6；每20步评估；比较 base、f≈30%、f≈50%；600步共同窗口，比较窗口400–600步；dropout 至少保留3条标准。base 在 Medical 上金分约 step 240 达峰31.2%后下降，代理升至72%，proxy−gold 差距从29%扩至44%；Science 金分从峰值下降22点。8B Science 上 f≈30% 金分+6.4、f≈50% +7.0；8B Medical +1.0/+2.0；4B Medical +0.7/+3.0；1.7B Medical +1.7/0.0；4B Science +5.3/+2.1；1.7B Science +2.4/+3.4。摘要称 OOD 金评判分最多提升 HealthBench-Hard 3%、ResearchQA 7%，降低两评判差距与过度给分，域内奖励接近基线，30–50% dropout 最佳，Block Dropout 在同 dropout 率下进一步提升。

**判断**：该方法以极低开销改变每步被奖励的标准子集，使任何单条标准都无法被稳定利用，从而缓解 rubric 奖励的可利用性。跨两个基准与 1.7B–8B 模型，dropout 提升 OOD 金评判分并降低两评判差距与过度给分，域内奖励接近基线，30–50% 为最佳区间，Block Dropout 在同 dropout 率下进一步改善。局限：金评判并非真实 ground truth，论文不解释差距大小；固定偏差不能制造或消除两曲线分歧；该测试不能排除金评判错误随策略变化；机制解释仅作动机直觉而非已确立机制；模型规模与最优 dropout 比例之间未建立一般关系；除 Medical 4B 外多为单次运行，仅覆盖一个策略族、三个尺寸、两个领域与一种 RL 算法，块 dropout 阈值未调优。

**原文摘要**：Rubric-based reinforcement learning, in which an LLM judge scores responses against prompt-specific criteria, has become a standard way to post-train language models on tasks without verifiable answers. The rubric, however, is only a proxy for response quality, and a policy trained against it long enough learns to exploit the difference. We train models with GRPO on medical and science rubrics and grade their responses on out-of-distribution (OOD) benchmarks throughout training with two judges: the training judge and a stronger OOD judge. The two scores first rise together and then diverge. The policy is learning to satisfy the training judge without writing better responses, which is a form of reward hacking. To mitigate it, we propose Rubric Dropout, which applies dropout to rubric criteria: at each training step, a random subset of each rubric's criteria is removed from the reward, so the rewarded subset of criteria is resampled at each step. All responses sampled for the same prompt are scored on the same subset, so their rewards remain comparable, and evaluation always uses the full rubric. We also introduce Block Dropout, which drops semantically similar criteria together so that a near duplicate of a dropped criterion cannot keep rewarding the same behavior. Across both benchmarks and models from 1.7B to 8B, dropout improves the gold judge's score on OOD benchmarks by up to 3 percent on HealthBench-Hard and 7 percent on ResearchQA, and it reduces both the gap between the two judges and over-crediting. It keeps in-domain reward close to the baseline, and dropout rates of 30-50 percent work best. Block Dropout improves the gold judge's OOD score further at the same dropout rate. Changing which criteria are rewarded at each step is a simple, inexpensive way to make rubric rewards harder to exploit.

## 注意力现象

### Hybrid Gated Attention

[arXiv:2608.11805v2](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.11805v2) · [论文 PDF](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2608.11805v2)

**作者**：Zekun Zhou；Ruobing Xie；Lanrui Wang；Weixuan Sun

**公告日期**：2026-10-15；**更新类型**：替换版本；**阅读版本**：v2

**标签**：主题=`注意力现象`；机构组合=`单位层级待核实`；覆盖面=`任务内通用`；任务类型=`语言模型注意力机制`

**作者单位**：Tencent Hunyuan；Peking University

**问题**：门控注意力虽能缓解注意力汇聚并增强注意力表达能力，但仍存在三方面局限：原始门控仅依赖注意力层前的原始输入X，忽略了注意力其他阶段的信息；逐元素门控引入大量额外参数与计算开销，效率有待提升；现有门控注意力仍存在不可忽略的BOS-token汇聚比例，训练稳定性不足。

**方法**：提出Hybrid Gated Attention（HyGA）框架，包含三种协同门控：X-gate以原始输入X为控制器做逐元素门控；H-gate以头特定SDPA输出Hi为控制器做逐元素门控，采用两层MLP加SiLU；C-gate以拼接后的全部头输出H为控制器做head-wise门控，捕捉跨头相关性。三者提供多源调制信号，覆盖头内与跨头交互。门融合采用加性耦合而非乘性耦合，避免多个sigmoid门叠加造成过度抑制与梯度减弱。对X-gate与H-gate的投影施加低秩矩阵分解，以中间维度d_int控制最大秩；并引入可学习attention sink提升训练稳定性。

**实验范围**：主实验在MoE-5B（约1B激活/5B总参数，64专家4激活，MLA，Muon优化器，500B tokens）与Qwen3-0.6B（dense，GQA，AdamW，约200B tokens）两种骨干上评测14个基准（CEval、CMMLU、MMLU、AGIEval、ARC、GPQA-Diamond、GSM8K、MATH、MBPP+、HellaSwag、PIQA、SIQA、Natural Questions、TriviaQA），取三次随机种子平均。MoE-5B上HyGA平均42.44，优于Gated attention的40.98（+1.46%），ARC 57.80对53.79、GSM8K 30.45对28.23、MBPP+ 39.77对34.92；训练损失在60k步约低0.012。Qwen3-0.6B上六基准平均30.56对29.44，200B tokens后训练损失约低0.008。消融显示C-gate、H-gate与完整HyGA平均增益分别为+0.49%、+1.05%、+1.46%。低秩压缩下以约29%的门控计算成本取得略好性能；门控参数与FLOPs完全匹配时14个下游基准平均提升1.18分。

**判断**：HyGA通过多阶段、多头视角的混合门控扩展了门控注意力的效果-效率帕累托前沿，在两种骨干与多类基准上一致优于经典Gated attention，并借助低秩分解与可学习attention sink同时改善效率与稳定性，BOS注意力分数持续降低。局限方面，原文未单列局限章节，仅指出Qwen3-0.6B因模型与训练token规模较小，部分基准结果可靠性较低，故只选六个较可靠基准报告差距；作者亦说明受算力限制主要在MoE-5B与Qwen上验证，未扩展至更大模型或更大token预算，也未验证线性、稀疏、混合注意力架构。

**原文摘要**：Gated attention is an effective approach to mitigate attention sinks and enhance the representational capacity of attention. To further extend its effectiveness-efficiency Pareto frontier, we propose a Hybrid Gated Attention (HyGA) framework that contains three types of gating strategies. Specifically, these gates leverage diverse information from multiple stages of attention, and collaboratively build element-wise/head-wise gating from multiple perspectives, capturing intra-head and cross-head information interactions. Through our hybrid gating components, HyGA could provide multi-source modulation signals, enabling more comprehensive control over information flow and improving the representational capacity of attention. We also introduce low-rank matrix decomposition and learnable attention sink to further enhance training efficiency and stability. In experiments, we evaluate HyGA on widely-used benchmarks based on different backbones. The experimental results show that our HyGA comprehensively improves both training loss and various downstream performances compared with Gated attention. HyGA has also been verified to achieve the best performance at different computation costs, with comprehensive model analyses for better understanding. The proposed HyGA sheds light on a more effective, efficient, and stable attention mechanism.
